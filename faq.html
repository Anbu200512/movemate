Pricing & Payment

Pricing is hourly per worker, so your total depends on how many workers you book and how many hours they work. Heavy-item handling is added as a flat fee per item. Use the pricing calculator for an exact figure before booking.

Extra time is billed at the same standard hourly rate, so there are no surprise premiums. Our crew will always confirm with you before continuing past the booked hours. You can adjust the booking in your dashboard up to two hours before the start time.

UPI, credit and debit cards, and net banking are all supported for online payment. You can also pay cash or by UPI directly to the crew at the end of a job. Corporate accounts can request monthly invoicing.

A small advance confirms the slot and holds your crew and time window. The balance is settled once the job is finished. The exact advance amount is shown on your booking summary before payment.

On Moving Day

Yes. Every mover is identity-verified and trained in safe lifting before joining a job. Crews also carry basic tools and moving blankets, and they wear uniform so you always know who is in your home.

Stairs are included at no extra charge. For very bulky items such as sofas, wardrobes, and refrigerators, tell us in advance so we can send the right crew size. If your building has a service lift, mention it in your booking notes to save time.

Our crews are covered by liability insurance, and any damage should be reported within 24 hours so we can document and settle it fairly. Photographing items before and after the move makes any claim much faster to resolve.

Clear walkways on both floors, empty and open any drawers, and keep fragile or high-value items packed yourself. Point out fragile surfaces like stairs, door frames, and glass doors before we start. A quick walkthrough with the crew helps everyone stay on the same page.

Packing & Protection

Our crews are happy to wrap and protect furniture with moving blankets, and we can supply boxes, bubble wrap, and tape at cost. Full packing and unpacking is billed by the hour like any other labor, so you stay in control of the budget.

A one-bedroom move usually takes four to six hours with two workers, while a three-bedroom home runs closer to eight. Elevators, stairs, and heavy items all add time, which is why we recommend confirming your hours rather than guessing.

Cancellations & Refunds

Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your start time. Inside 24 hours the advance is non-refundable, since the crew has already been scheduled and turned down other work. You can reschedule instead with no charge if you tell us early.

Refunds are initiated within 48 hours of approval and reach your original payment method in five to seven business days, depending on your bank or UPI provider.

We allow a fifteen-minute grace window, and our support line stays reachable during every booking. If a crew cannot reach you, we send a replacement at no cost and extend the booked hours to cover the delay.
