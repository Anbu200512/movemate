What every booking includes

No tiers and no upsells at the door. Every MoveMate job starts from the same baseline, agreed before the crew arrives.

  • Checked crews. Every mover is identity-verified and trained in safe lifting before joining a job.

  • Covered on the job. Liability insurance applies to every booking, so damage can be reported and settled fairly.

  • Their own gear. Tools and moving blankets come with the crew, so nothing extra is billed on the day.

  • Two-hour minimum. Then you are billed by the hour in one-hour increments, with no booking fee.
